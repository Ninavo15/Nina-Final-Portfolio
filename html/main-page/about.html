About Me

My full name is Nguyen Hoai Phuong Vo, but you can just call me Nina. I was born and raised in Vietnam.

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Interactive Arts and Technology at Simon Fraser University.

I'm a designer and developer who loves turning ideas into reality. I enjoy building things from scratch, making them look great, and bringing them to life through design and technology. Whether it's designing an interface or figuring out how things work behind the scenes, I love exploring both the creative and technical sides of a project.

People often describe me as a people person. I'm friendly, curious, adaptable, and a fast learner. I enjoy collaborating with others, sharing ideas, solving problems, and learning from different perspectives. I believe good communication, teamwork, and a positive attitude can turn great ideas into even better experiences.

For me, it's all about creating, connecting, and continuously learning!
