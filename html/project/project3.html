DormMate

A mobile app for residence life. Students report maintenance issues and answer building notices, and residence staff manage them from one dashboard.

The Problem

Dorm maintenance runs on emails and paper notices. Students don't know whether anyone saw their report or when it will be fixed. Staff get every request in the same inbox, so a flooded bathroom can sit behind a slow Wi-Fi complaint. Notices like pest inspections go up on doors, and changing the time means tracking someone down.

The Idea

DormMate puts all of this in one app with two sides: one for students and one for staff. Students report issues with photos, set priority with a limited supply of urgent tokens, and track each fix from Pending to Completed. Staff manage tickets and post announcements from one dashboard.

How It Works

Student

  • Opens a dashboard showing their room, open issues, and new announcements.
  • Reports an issue by choosing up to two categories (electrical, plumbing, damage, parking, Wi-Fi, other), describing it, and taking photos with the camera.
  • Spends a token to set priority: red for urgent (4 per student) or blue for normal (10 per student). The limit stops students from marking everything urgent.
  • Follows the report on a tracker: Pending → In Progress → Completed.
  • Reads announcements, acknowledges them, or requests a different time for things like a pest inspection.

Staff

  • Sees a dashboard of incoming work.
  • Manages all reports in one list, with colour-coded status badges.
  • Opens a ticket and moves it between Pending, In Progress, and Completed.
  • Writes announcements with a category, a priority, a date, a time window, and a confirmation step before sending.

My Contribution

I designed the app's visual design and owned the front end: how every screen looks, feels, and moves.

Design & UI

  • UI polish across the whole app — restyled every student and staff screen so they share the same spacing, colours, cards, and headers, from the welcome and login screens through to the staff ticket view.
  • Announcements and acknowledgement — designed the student announcement feed and detail screen, with an “Urgent” badge, an Acknowledge button, and a path to the time-change request.
  • Report and progress screens — designed the report-history list and the step-by-step progress tracker, so students can see exactly where their issue is.
  • Icons and visual assets — added the category icons, the red and blue token graphics, and the map pin.

Features & Engineering

  • Scheduling: time-change requests — built the flow where a student picks a new date and a start and end time to request a different slot for a building notice. Staff use the same date and time-window pickers to schedule announcements.

Tech Stack

Front end

  • React Native + TypeScript
  • Expo Router — file-based, typed routes
  • DateTimePicker — date and time-window scheduling
  • react-native-modal — confirmation and detail sheets
  • Expo Haptics — touch feedback

Backend & device

  • Firebase Auth — sign-in for students and staff
  • Cloud Firestore — reports, tickets, announcements
  • AsyncStorage — local storage
  • expo-image-picker — camera photos for reports
  • react-native-maps + expo-location
